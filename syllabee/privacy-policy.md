# Privacy policy: Stavebi, Syllabee, Складарик and Слогарик

**Effective date:** 16 October 2026

**Developer:** forio.dk@gmail.com

## Who the app is for

Stavebi, Syllabee, Складарик and Слогарик are one reading app in four languages, for children under 13 who are
learning to read Danish (Stavebi), English (Syllabee), Ukrainian (Складарик) or Russian (Слогарик), and for the parents
who choose it for them. This policy covers all four: they collect, store and share exactly the same things, and "the
app" below means any of them.

## What the app itself collects

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in and no server of its own, and nowhere to type a name or any other personal
information. It keeps its settings on the device: the chosen game, whether Syllables blurs its pictures, how many pairs
Memo deals, how Build a word cuts a word, which countries Countries deals, which shapes Shapes deals, and whether the
full version has been bought. None of this records what a child has done. It leaves the device only in Android's own
backup to the device owner's Google account, where that backup is turned on; the developer never receives it.
Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android's settings, deletes it.

## Advertising

The app shows a banner served by Google AdMob. Every ad request is marked as directed at children, so Google applies its
protections for children: no personalised ads, no remarketing, and ad content limited to the rating suitable for all
ages (G). This holds in every country, so the app asks no one for consent to personalised advertising, in the European
Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland included. The app does not use the Android advertising ID; the permissions that
would allow it, and those for Android's Privacy Sandbox ad APIs, are removed from the app.

To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's ad SDK processes the device's IP address (which can indicate
an approximate location), information about the ads shown and tapped, app and SDK performance diagnostics, and the
app set ID. This is sent to Google, encrypted in transit. How Google uses and keeps it is described in Google's privacy
policy and in "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services":

- https://policies.google.com/privacy
- https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## Purchases

«Full version» («Fuld version», «Повна версія», «Полная версия») is a one-time purchase through Google Play that removes
the ads and unlocks the rest of the themes and shapes. Google Play processes the payment; the app receives only whether
the purchase was made, and never sees payment details.

## Speech

Words are spoken by the device's own text-to-speech engine. The app sends it only the word or letter to say.

## Parents

Purchases are made through the Google Play account on the device, so Google Play's purchase authentication settings
and Family Link controls apply. For any question about this policy, contact forio.dk@gmail.com.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
