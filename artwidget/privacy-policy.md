Art Widget (`dk.forio.artwidget`) shows artworks and their details from Wikidata and images from Wikimedia Commons.

**What the app itself collects.** Nothing. It has no accounts, sends no analytics of its own and stores only the
current artwork, its image and your language choice, on your phone.

**Advertising.** The app shows one banner from Google AdMob. Google's ads SDK may collect your device's advertising
ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, information about the app and your device, and how you interact
with ads, to show, measure and personalise ads and to prevent fraud. In the European Economic Area, the United
Kingdom and Switzerland the app asks for your consent first through Google's consent form, and Settings → Privacy
options lets you change your choice at any time. Google's use of this data is described at
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

**Purchases.** The full version is bought through Google Play, which handles the payment under its own privacy
policy, https://policies.google.com/privacy. The app learns only whether you own it, and remembers that on your phone.

**Wikimedia.** Images are downloaded from Wikimedia Commons, and Wikipedia articles open in the app. Wikimedia
receives your IP address and the pages you open, as it does for any visitor; see
https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Privacy_policy.

**Children.** The app is not directed at children under 13.

**Contact.** https://forio-dk.github.io/README.md
